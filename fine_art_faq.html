Frequently Asked Questions

❓ General Questions
What is the Fine Art Prompt Refiner?
A creative tool that transforms rough ideas into museum-quality AI art prompts. It generates structured briefs with character descriptions, technical specs, mood analysis, and 5 alternative prompt variations—all tailored to your chosen art style and generation model.
Do I need an account or API key?
No. The app is completely standalone and works offline. Everything saves locally on your device. You only need an API key if you want to generate images using Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, etc.—but those are separate from this tool.
How do I use this tool?
  1. Enter a creative idea (rough description is fine)
  2. Choose a drawing style (Realistic, Oil Painting, Chiaroscuro, etc.)
  3. Select a generation model (Midjourney, DALL-E, Flux, etc.)
  4. Add negative prompts (optional—what to avoid)
  5. Click "Refine to Gallery Brief"
  6. Get a detailed brief + 5 character/style variations
  7. Copy any prompt and paste into your AI generator
What's saved on my device?
Your creative idea, selected style, chosen model, and negative prompt settings. All stored locally in your browser—nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser cache, this data resets.
✍️ Prompt Writing Tips
What makes a good creative idea to input?
Be descriptive but not rigid. Include:
  • Visual mood (contemplative, dramatic, ethereal)
  • Lighting ideas (golden hour, candlelit, shadows)
  • Clothing/styling hints (draped linen, bohemian, elegant)
  • Emotional undercurrent (quiet dignity, raw power, vulnerability)
✓ GOOD: "Contemplative woman in golden light, draped linen, quiet dignity, warm amber glow"

✗ AVOID: "pretty girl"
Can I use real people's names?
It depends on your generator:
  • Midjourney & Flux: ✅ Yes, they handle public figures well
  • DALL-E 3: ❌ No, it rejects real public figures
  • Stable Diffusion: ✅ Generally allows it
Safe alternative: Use descriptive language instead.
Instead of: "Marilyn Monroe costume"
Say: "glamorous 1950s starlet aesthetic, platinum waves, red lips"
Can I use copyrighted characters?
Same as above:
  • Midjourney & Flux: ✅ Permissive with IP
  • DALL-E 3: ❌ Strict on copyright
  • Stable Diffusion: ✅ Generally allows it
Safe alternative: Describe the character concept without naming them.
Instead of: "Wonder Woman"
Say: "powerful warrior in gold and crimson, iconic stance, classical beauty"
What should I put in the Negative Prompt?
Things you DON'T want in the image. Pre-filled defaults are solid:
bad anatomy, extra fingers, distortion, blur, dull lighting, overexposure
Add more if needed:
  • Low quality, pixelated, AI artifacts
  • Oversaturated, washed out, flat lighting
  • Distorted faces, weird hands, anatomically impossible
✅ What Works / ❌ What Doesn't
What SHOULD I include in prompts?
  • ✅ Art historical references (Rembrandt, Baroque, Pre-Raphaelite, Chiaroscuro)
  • ✅ Emotional descriptors (melancholy, reverence, quiet contemplation, raw power)
  • ✅ Lighting concepts (golden hour, candlelit, Rembrandt lighting, dramatic shadows)
  • ✅ Texture & fabric details (silk, linen, velvet, translucent)
  • ✅ Color palettes (warm amber, earth tones, jewel tones, cool silver)
  • ✅ Camera/technical terms (shallow depth of field, 85mm, cinematic, sharp focus)
What should I AVOID?
  • ❌ Minors in any suggestive context
  • ❌ Graphic violence, gore, self-harm
  • ❌ Sexual/NSFW content
  • ❌ Hateful stereotypes or slurs
  • ❌ Medical/pharmaceutical instructions
  • ❌ Contradictory instructions ("ethereal yet heavy and dark")
  • ❌ Too many technical jargon terms at once
Why did my prompt come back blank or rejected?
Common causes:
  • Named real public figure (use descriptive language instead)
  • Copyrighted character on DALL-E (use concept description instead)
  • Prohibited content (violence, NSFW, minors)
  • Too vague input (give more visual details)
Solution: Rephrase using descriptive, artistic language instead of proper names.
🎨 Styles & Generation Models
Which drawing style should I choose?
Depends on your vision:
  • Realistic: Photographic precision
  • Oil Painting: Textured, Old Masters sensibility
  • Watercolor: Soft, ethereal, fluid
  • Chiaroscuro: Dramatic light/shadow (Rembrandt-inspired)
  • Raphealite: Pre-Raphaelite richness, intricate detail, jewel tones
  • Baroque: Ornate grandeur, theatrical drama
  • Cinematic: Film-quality lighting and composition
  • Surreal: Dreamlike, impossible elements
Which generation model should I use?
  • Midjourney 6.1: Excellent all-around, handles real people/IP well, cinematic
  • DALL-E 3: Great narratives, strict on public figures, good detail
  • Stable Diffusion: Customizable, permissive with figures, community-friendly
  • Flux: Fast, high fidelity, strong on realism
  • Leonardo AI: Excellent texture and fine detail
  • Adobe Firefly: Adobe ecosystem, good for retouching
For best results with named figures/IP: Use Midjourney or Flux
What are the 5 character variations?
When you refine a prompt, you get 5 different interpretations of the same character concept—each with:
  • Different hair/appearance details
  • Different facial expression
  • Different art style
This lets you explore multiple "looks" of the same core idea. Copy whichever resonates most and generate with your chosen model.
🔧 Troubleshooting
My prompts aren't saving. What's wrong?
Check if your browser allows local storage:
  • Private/Incognito mode disables storage
  • Check browser settings for storage permissions
  • Clear cache and try again
If nothing works, just take screenshots of your briefs instead.
Can I use this on mobile?
Yes! It's responsive and works on phones/tablets. Just download the HTML file and open it in your mobile browser. All features work the same.
Can I edit the prompts after they're generated?
Yes! Copy the prompt into a text editor or directly into your AI generator and tweak it. The variations are suggestions—feel free to customize, combine, or rewrite them.
How do I download my brief?
After refining, click the "Download Brief" button. It saves as a .txt file with all your sections (Headline, Subject, Technical, Prompts, etc.).
💡 Tips & Best Practices
Start Vague, Then Refine

First pass: "contemplative portrait with dramatic lighting"
Second pass: Add specifics after seeing variations

Mix Styles & Concepts

The 5 variations show you possibilities. Copy one, tweak it, combine elements from multiple variations into a custom prompt.

Use Negative Prompts Strategically

Start with defaults. After first generation, add specific things you don't want. Iterate.

Reference Art Movements

Say "Rembrandt lighting" instead of "dramatic shadows." Say "Pre-Raphaelite detail" instead of "intricate." Generators understand art history.

Test on Different Generators

Same prompt generates differently on Midjourney vs. DALL-E vs. Flux. If one doesn't work, try another.
